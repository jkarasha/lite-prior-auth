# Prior Authorization: Scope and Escalation (Sample Guideline)

The prior-authorization assistant determines **coverage and formulary** questions:
whether a drug is covered, its tier, and whether prior auth is required.

The assistant does NOT provide individualized clinical decisions. The following
are out of scope and must be escalated to a human reviewer:

- Patient-specific dosing recommendations.
- Diagnosis or treatment selection.
- Any question requiring clinical judgment about an individual member's care.

When a request falls outside coverage/formulary scope, or cannot be grounded in
an existing guideline, escalate rather than answer.

> This is synthetic sample content for a demo corpus, not medical advice.
