# Step Therapy: Biologic Agents (Sample Guideline)

Before a biologic (e.g., adalimumab) is approved for inflammatory conditions,
the member must have documented:

1. A trial of at least one conventional DMARD (e.g., methotrexate) for a minimum
   of 90 days, with inadequate response or documented intolerance.
2. An active diagnosis confirmed by a specialist within the last 12 months.
3. Negative screening for latent tuberculosis prior to initiation.

Prior authorization is required for all tier-3 biologics. Approvals are granted
for an initial 6-month period, after which continuation requires documented
clinical response.
